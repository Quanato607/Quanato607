# Hi, I'm Shenghao Zhu

I am a visiting student at the University of Michigan and hold a bachelor's degree in Computer Science and Technology from Hangzhou Dianzi University.

My research focuses on medical AI, with interests in multimodal learning, reinforcement learning, and interactive medical agents. I study how models can learn from incomplete medical data and how agents can gather and reason over clinical evidence through multi-turn interactions.

My current work explores patient simulation and reinforcement learning for interactive diagnosis, building on my previous research in missing-modality medical image analysis and knowledge distillation. I aim to develop reliable AI systems that can support clinical reasoning and decision-making.
