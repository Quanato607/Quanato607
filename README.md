I am a **Medical AI Research Intern at Ant Group**, with a B.S. in Computer Science and Technology from **Hangzhou Dianzi University**. My research focuses on learning from incomplete medical data and developing agents that gather and reason over clinical evidence.

**Ph.D. applicant · Fall 2027.** Learn more about my research and experience on my [academic website](https://quanato607.github.io/).

## Research interests

- **Robust multimodal learning:** medical image analysis when modalities are missing or degraded.
- **Knowledge distillation:** transferring information across modalities for brain tumor segmentation.
- **Multi-turn medical agents:** integrating heterogeneous evidence and refining reasoning through interaction.
